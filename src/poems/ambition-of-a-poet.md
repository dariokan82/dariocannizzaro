---
title: Ambition of a Poet
year: 2014
order: 1
description: A poem by Dario Cannizzaro.
---

I used to write my name in the sand
(waves would steal it and bring it to the bottom of the sea)

or on my father's car
cleaning it out of the windshield dust;

In the bathroom of our high school,
(where me and my friends would go for a smoke)

on the ancient stones
that made up the *anfiteatro*
(forgive me for I have sinned);

at the end of my poems,
my scripts, my books.

I'd smirk at how others would pronounce it when I'm dead
a thousand years from now--

When my clothes will be dust
and I myself
ashes

nothing'll keep my name
like my lover's heart.
